Privacy Policy for Digital Calculator

Last updated: September 2026

Digital Calculator ("the App") is a calculator and customer ledger application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information You Enter

The App allows you to enter information such as customer names, phone numbers, product details, prices, payments, dues, and other information needed for calculation and customer ledger records.

Customer ledger information is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage. The developer does not operate a server that stores your customer ledger records.

2. Contacts

The App may allow you to select a phone contact using the Android system contact picker. The contact picker is used to help fill contact information into the App.

The App does not request access to your entire contacts list. Only the contact information that you choose through the system contact picker may be made available to the App.

If you save information obtained from a selected contact into a customer record, that information may be stored locally on your device as part of that customer record.

3. WhatsApp and SMS

The App may provide options to send reminders using WhatsApp or SMS. When you choose one of these options, the App opens the corresponding external application.

Messages are handled by the selected external service. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information as described by Google.

Advertising and measurement may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent, applicable laws, and Google's systems.

For more information about Google's advertising technologies and privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy and advertising consent choices.

Where required, users may be shown a consent message and may manage available advertising privacy choices through the App.

6. Data Security

Information stored by the App locally remains on your device unless you choose to share it through another application or service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Customer ledger information stored locally remains on your device until you delete the records through the App or remove the App's stored data/uninstall the App.

Information shared with external applications such as WhatsApp or SMS is handled according to those services' own policies.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of maintaining an online account or profile.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent technology. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
